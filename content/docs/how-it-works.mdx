## The quote

When you set `amount`, the modal asks for exactly that much of your destination token, and the payer is told exactly what to send. The payer’s amount is rounded up only when the increase is 0.25% or less.

Without `amount`, the payer enters how much they want to send and your app receives the quoted output.

## Deposit and settlement

The modal shows one deposit address, with a QR code and a copy button. The payer sends exactly the quoted amount from their own wallet. Uniport routes the funds and settles them to your `recipient` on the destination chain, while the modal tracks the payment’s status.

When the payment completes, the modal links to the transaction on a block explorer and the button calls `onSuccess`.
