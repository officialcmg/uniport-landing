## Naming

Destination tokens are named `{chain}{SYMBOL}`: `optimismUSDC`, `arbitrumUSDC`, `solanaSOL`, `bitcoinBTC`. Legacy short aliases such as `arbUSDC` and `ethUSDC` still work, but the canonical names are the ones to ship against.

## Source chains

Ethereum, Base, Optimism, Arbitrum, Polygon, Solana, Sui, NEAR, Bitcoin, Avalanche, BNB Chain, Aptos, TON, Tron, Stellar, Cardano, XRP Ledger, Dogecoin, Litecoin, Bitcoin Cash, Starknet, Berachain, Zcash, Scroll, Gnosis, Monad, Hyperliquid, Plasma, Robinhood Chain, X Layer, Dash and ADI Chain.

## Display amounts

Cross-chain quotes often land on long decimals. The modal rounds the amounts it displays to 6 significant digits so they stay readable. The actual quote, deposit and settlement amounts are never changed.
