<H2 id="amount">Fixed amounts</H2>

With `amount`, the modal asks for exactly that much of your destination token. The payer’s quote is rounded up only when the increase is 0.25% or less.

Without it, the payer enters how much they send and your app receives the quoted output.

<H2 id="refunds">Refund address</H2>

If a payment can’t complete, funds go back to the payer’s refund address on the chain they paid from. The payer enters it in the modal, or you prefill it with `refundAddress`. With a fixed `amount` there’s no refund address: refunds go to Uniport’s NEAR Intents account. See [Refunds](/docs/refunds#fixed-amounts).

<Callout title="EVM chains only:">a blank refund address defaults to Uniport’s address, <C>uniport.eth</C>. On Solana, Sui, Bitcoin and other non-EVM chains the payer has to enter one.</Callout>
