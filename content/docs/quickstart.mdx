<Step n={3} id="app-id">Get your App ID</Step>

Sign in to the [dashboard](/dashboard), create an app, and add the website it runs on. Copy the App ID it gives you into the `appId` prop.

The App ID is public, so it is safe in your site’s code. What it controls is which websites may use it, and it is how your payments show up in your dashboard. On `localhost` the button works without one, so you can try Uniport before you sign up.
