## Do I need an API key?

No. The SDK calls Uniport’s hosted backend, which keeps the routing API key server-side. Nothing secret ships in your client.

## Where do funds settle?

To the `recipient` address you pass, on the chain and token named by `destinationToken`. With `optimismUSDC`, your app receives USDC on Optimism.

## What happens if a payment fails?

Funds go back to the payer’s refund address on the chain they paid from. With a fixed `amount`, refunds go to Uniport’s NEAR Intents account instead, and the payer contacts Uniport to get them back. See [Refunds](/docs/refunds).

## Which chains need a memo?

Stellar. The modal shows the memo right next to the deposit address. See [Memo chains](/docs/memo-chains).

## What does Uniport cost?

Uniport takes 0.4% of each deposit. With a fixed `amount`, Uniport also rounds what the payer sends up to a friendlier number, by at most 0.25%, and keeps the difference.

## Which frameworks does it work with?

Any React 18 or later app: Next.js, Vite, Remix and others.
