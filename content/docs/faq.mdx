## Do I need an API key?

No secret key. You create a free App ID in the [dashboard](/dashboard) and pass it to the button as `appId`. It is public, so nothing secret ships in your client. On `localhost` the button works without one.

## What does the App ID protect?

It says which payments are yours, and it only works in a browser on the websites you list for the app. It does not stop someone calling the API from a script, where the usual rate limits apply. If you only ever take payments to a few addresses, list them in the app’s settings: the app will then refuse a payment to any other address.

## How do I know a payment really happened?

Don’t rely on `onSuccess` alone; it runs in the payer’s browser. Have your server [confirm the payment](/docs/confirm-payment) before you ship goods or grant access.

## Where do funds settle?

To the `recipient` address you pass, on the chain and token named by `destinationToken`. With `optimismUSDC`, your app receives USDC on Optimism.

## What happens if a payment fails?

Funds go back to the payer’s refund address on the chain they paid from. With a fixed `amount`, refunds go to Uniport’s NEAR Intents account instead, and the payer contacts Uniport to get them back. See [Refunds](/docs/refunds).

## Which chains need a memo?

Stellar. The modal shows the memo right next to the deposit address. See [Memo chains](/docs/memo-chains).

## What does Uniport cost?

Uniport takes 0.4% of each deposit. With a fixed `amount`, Uniport also rounds what the payer sends up to a friendlier number, by at most 0.01%, and keeps the difference.

## Which frameworks does it work with?

Any React 18 or later app: Next.js, Vite, Remix and others.
