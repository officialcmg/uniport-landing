## 0.0.11

October 27, 2026

- When your app sets `amount`, the payer can no longer change it in the modal. Before, a payer could edit a checkout amount and pay less.
- `onSuccess` runs in the payer's browser. Before granting goods or access, confirm on your server that the recipient received the amount.

## 0.0.10

October 27, 2026

- 17 more tokens on existing chains: USD1, cbBTC, wstETH, XAUT and AURORA on Ethereum; VVV and KAITO on Base; USD1 and PENGU on Solana; USDC and USDT on Aptos; ASTER on BNB Chain; USDT0 on Optimism and Berachain; OKB on X Layer; RHEA and AURORA on NEAR.
- Removed GMX and BRETT, which no longer have liquidity on 1Click.

## 0.0.9

October 24, 2026

- Eight new chains: Gnosis, Monad, Hyperliquid, Plasma, Robinhood Chain, X Layer, Dash and ADI Chain.
- Corrected token and chain icons, including DAI, AAVE, SAFE, GMX, cbBTC, WIF, wNEAR, BERA and Monad.
- Arbitrum USDT now shows as USDT0 and TON as GRAM, matching their current names. Token names such as `arbitrumUSDT` and `tonTON` are unchanged.
- Receipt links use the block explorer URL reported by 1Click when the backend provides it.

## 0.0.7

July 14, 2026

- The chain list in the modal is reordered, with Stellar first.

## 0.0.6

July 14, 2026

- Block explorer links for completed payments.
- On EVM chains, a blank refund address defaults to Uniport’s address, resolved from `uniport.eth`.
- Displayed amounts are rounded to 6 significant digits (display only).
- The memo is shown next to the deposit address on chains that need one.
- Stellar and Scroll support.
