## 0.1.2

November 16, 2026

- **Search in the modal.** The network list has a search box, and so does the token list on networks with more than 8 tokens.
- **Powered by Uniport** appears at the bottom of the modal.
- Payments to and from Polygon work again.

## 0.1.1

November 16, 2026

- When you set a fixed `amount`, the payer’s amount is rounded up to a shorter number. That rounding is now capped at 0.01% (one cent per $100), down from 0.25%. The payer sends at most five significant digits.

## 0.1.0

November 16, 2026

This release changes how you set up the button. A deployed site now needs an App ID.

- **App IDs.** Create a free app in the [dashboard](/dashboard) and pass its ID as the new `appId` prop (or once with `configureUniport({ appId })`). On `localhost` the button still works without one. The App ID is public; it says which payments are yours and which websites may take them.
- **A dashboard.** See each app’s payments and volume, choose the websites allowed to use its App ID, and optionally limit the addresses it can be paid to.
- **Confirm a payment on your server.** `onSuccess` now also returns `depositAddress`, `depositMemo` and `correlationId`. Send the deposit address to your server and check it with `GET https://api.uniport.dev/v1/payments/{depositAddress}`. See [Confirm a payment](/docs/confirm-payment).
- New error codes for setup problems: `APP_ID_REQUIRED`, `APP_NOT_FOUND`, `ORIGIN_NOT_ALLOWED`, `RECIPIENT_NOT_ALLOWED`, plus `RATE_LIMITED`. The payer sees a neutral message and the reason is logged to the browser console.
- `referral` is removed from `QuoteOptions`.
- Versions before 0.0.12 no longer work: they use a backend that has been shut down.

## 0.0.12

November 15, 2026

- The SDK now uses the new backend at `api.uniport.dev`.

## 0.0.11

October 27, 2026

- When your app sets `amount`, the payer can no longer change it in the modal. Before, a payer could edit a checkout amount and pay less.
- `onSuccess` runs in the payer's browser. Before granting goods or access, confirm on your server that the recipient received the amount.

## 0.0.10

October 27, 2026

- 17 more tokens on existing chains: USD1, cbBTC, wstETH, XAUT and AURORA on Ethereum; VVV and KAITO on Base; USD1 and PENGU on Solana; USDC and USDT on Aptos; ASTER on BNB Chain; USDT0 on Optimism and Berachain; OKB on X Layer; RHEA and AURORA on NEAR.
- Removed GMX and BRETT, which no longer have liquidity on 1Click.

## 0.0.9

October 24, 2026

- Eight new chains: Gnosis, Monad, Hyperliquid, Plasma, Robinhood Chain, X Layer, Dash and ADI Chain.
- Corrected token and chain icons, including DAI, AAVE, SAFE, GMX, cbBTC, WIF, wNEAR, BERA and Monad.
- Arbitrum USDT now shows as USDT0 and TON as GRAM, matching their current names. Token names such as `arbitrumUSDT` and `tonTON` are unchanged.
- Receipt links use the block explorer URL reported by 1Click when the backend provides it.

## 0.0.7

July 14, 2026

- The chain list in the modal is reordered, with Stellar first.

## 0.0.6

July 14, 2026

- Block explorer links for completed payments.
- On EVM chains, a blank refund address defaults to Uniport’s address, resolved from `uniport.eth`.
- Displayed amounts are rounded to 6 significant digits (display only).
- The memo is shown next to the deposit address on chains that need one.
- Stellar and Scroll support.
