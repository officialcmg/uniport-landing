## 0.0.7

July 14, 2026

- The chain list in the modal is reordered, with Stellar first.

## 0.0.6

July 14, 2026

- Block explorer links for completed payments.
- On EVM chains, a blank refund address defaults to Uniport’s address, resolved from `uniport.eth`.
- Displayed amounts are rounded to 6 significant digits (display only).
- The memo is shown next to the deposit address on chains that need one.
- Stellar and Scroll support.
