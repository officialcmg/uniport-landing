## Theme

`theme` sets the button and the modal to `"light"` (the default) or `"dark"`. Use `"dark"` on dark backgrounds.

## className and disabled

`className` is added to the button so you can apply your own styles. `disabled` turns the button off, for example until your form has a recipient.
